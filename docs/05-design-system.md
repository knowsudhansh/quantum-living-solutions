# Design System: Quantum Living Solutions

## 1. Creative Direction & Color Philosophy
The color system reflects natural architectural illumination. We completely reject artificial glowing designs. We utilize three cohesive palettes representing times of day.

### Color Palettes
- **Day Palette (Morning / Bright Light)**:
  - Background: HSL(30, 20%, 97%) — soft sand off-white.
  - Text: HSL(210, 20%, 15%) — deep slate charcoal.
  - Accent: HSL(35, 30%, 45%) — warm bronze.
- **Evening Palette (Warm Twilight / Transition)**:
  - Background: HSL(20, 15%, 15%) — architectural dusk terracotta grey.
  - Text: HSL(40, 30%, 95%) — soft parchment white.
  - Accent: HSL(25, 60%, 50%) — copper clay.
- **Night Palette (Midnight / Security)**:
  - Background: HSL(220, 25%, 7%) — deep obsidian black.
  - Text: HSL(210, 15%, 85%) — silver grey.
  - Accent: HSL(210, 80%, 60%) — steel blue (not neon).

---

## 2. Typography Principles
- **Headings**: Serif or architectural sans-serif (e.g., *Playfair Display* or *Outfit*). Heading scale:
  - H1: `3.5rem` / `line-height: 1.1`
  - H2: `2.25rem` / `line-height: 1.2`
  - H3: `1.5rem` / `line-height: 1.3`
- **Body**: High-readability sans-serif (e.g., *Inter* or *Roboto*).
  - Body: `1rem` (16px) / `line-height: 1.6`. Letter spacing: normal.

---

## 3. Materials & Grid Systems
- **Materials**: Matte textures, natural concrete, wood grain images, brushed metal accents.
- **Glassmorphism Limit**: Restrict `backdrop-filter: blur` strictly to floating header navigation bars. Never use blur elements for text containers.
- **Grid Layout**: 12-column architectural grid on desktop. Margin: `8%` left/right. Gutters: `24px`. Alignment must feel like an editorial magazine.

---

## 4. Strict Anti-Vibe-Coding Policy

> [!WARNING]
> Future developers and AI agents must strictly adhere to these visual restrictions. Failure to do so will result in code rejection.

### Prohibited Patterns
1. **No Neon / RGB Gradients**: Do not use neon purple/pink/blue gradient borders, buttons, or backdrops.
2. **No Arbitrary Cards**: Do not wrap every item in a rounded translucent glass box. Use whitespace for separation.
3. **No Meaningless Particles**: Do not add floating dots, grid background loops, or stars that have no connection to actual systems.
4. **No Floating 3D Objects**: Avoid unrelated floating spheres, torus geometries, or abstract shapes spinning in space.
5. **No Fake Metrics**: Do not include fake counts like "99% Customer Satisfaction" or "10M+ Connected Hours" unless backed by database analytics.
6. **No AI Marketing Copy**: Avoid buzzwords like "Revolutionizing the future," "Next-gen synergy," or "AI-powered paradigms." Keep copy technical, premium, and plain-spoken.
7. **No Glowing Dropshadows**: Do not apply text-shadow glow or box-shadow glow except to show actual light emitters (lamps/sensors) matching the storyboard logic.
8. **No Template Layouts**: Avoid the "3-card features grid" directly under a massive hero title. Use off-center editorial layouts.
