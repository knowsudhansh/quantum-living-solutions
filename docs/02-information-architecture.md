## 2. Page Specifications & Content Focus

### Public Pages
- **Home (`/`)**: Cinematic day-to-night scroll journey. Core CTA: "Book an Experience".
- **Solutions (Hub & Sub-pages)**: Explain utility, hardware integration details, aesthetics, and user benefits. Avoid technical jargon without practical context.
- **Experience Room (`/experience`)**: WebGL/fallback space selector allowing visitors to toggle system presets.
- **Projects / Case Studies (`/projects`)**: Before/after automation diagrams, spatial photography, system topology, client testimonies.
- **Book a Demo (`/book-demo`)**: Calendar scheduler, budget calculator, checkout integration.

### Trust, Legal, and Compliance Pages
- **Refund & Cancellation Policy**: Strict 24-hour advance cancellation window for fully refundable bookings, partial refund mechanics, and processing timeframes (5-7 business days).
- **Payment Disclaimer**: Detailed text describing billing entities, Razorpay tokenization standards, and security compliance statement.
- **Privacy Policy**: GDPR/CCPA & local data standards compliance, tracking disclosures, and opt-out routes.
- **Terms of Service**: Acceptable use, booking rules, property entry terms, and disclaimer of liability for custom system recommendations.
